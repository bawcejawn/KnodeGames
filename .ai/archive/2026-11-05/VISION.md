## Mission
Give Koinos players a reason to play every day: fair, free-to-try prediction games about the chain itself — its producers, blocks and races — backed by our own block-producing nodes, where staked VHP keeps working for the network while players compete. — drafted from the owner's project description; ❓ owner to confirm the wording.

## Who it's for
- **Koinos holders with VHP** who want their stake to earn while they play (staked boards and the vault) — D-018.
- **Newcomers and guests** who can play free, with no wallet funds and no Mana of their own, through the sponsor and the guest wallet — D-009, D-053.
- **Block producers**, who are the subject of the games, play on equal terms, and can show their own names — D-030, D-057.

## Principles
1. **Funds safety first.** Funds must never be at risk. Only the vault holds tokens; every staked VHP is withdrawable at all times; a withdrawals-only emergency build is always ready — D-003, D-016, D-021.
2. **Ship the best version, not the fastest.** Nothing is announced until it is built, audited and ready (D-064); every contract upgrade passes the owner's audits (D-017).
3. **Fair and open.** Our own nodes play in the same open market as everyone else's, and we say so on the page — D-051, D-057, D-059. Rules are fixed before anyone can pick; anti-gaming checks protect honest players — D-055, D-058.
4. **Nothing should feel rushed.** Picks can be made well ahead and changed until each game's lock — D-059, D-051.
5. **Simple to use.** Stake, withdraw, move and claim take a couple of taps; the staking hub shows where you're staked and your share — D-031.
6. **Free to play, free to run.** The sponsor pays players' Mana; hosting costs nothing — D-005, D-043, D-053.
7. **Explain everything.** Every number is explained in plain words — D-059.

## What Knode Games is not
- Not a place where funds can be locked or lost to a bad upgrade — D-016, D-021.
- Not a pay-to-host service: no paid servers, no private repo — D-005, D-043.
- Not a promise that our nodes stay out of games — every node meeting the thresholds can be picked — D-051.
