# Knode Games — Founder Intent

> **Purpose:** why the founder built Knode Games, so every future decision can be checked against it. When a choice isn't covered by `RULES.md` or `DECISIONS.md`, ask: *does this serve the intent below?*
> **Last updated:** 2026-11-03
> **Sources:** the founder's own words across the project chats (👤) and recorded decisions (D-xxx). Drafted lines were confirmed or reworded by the founder on 3 Nov.

## Why Knode Games exists
- **To give Koinos a reason to show up every day.** Simple, fun games about the chain itself — who signs the next block, which producer lasts, who wins the race — that bring players back daily and drive real activity on Koinos. 👤
- **To let VHP holders earn while they play.** A player's stake keeps producing blocks inside the vault; the games decide who shares the rewards. 👤
- **To grow the network with real participants.** More traffic for the site and the chain — but only from players who contribute VHP, not spam. 👤
- **Because the founder is a Koinos block producer.** Knode Games grew out of running nodes: it started as one weekly prediction game (Kprediction → Knode) and became a platform of node-based games (Knode Games, 27 Oct). 👤
- It is also the founder's first coding project — built to learn as it grows. 👤

## Why Koinos
- **The founder produces blocks on Koinos** and knows the chain from the inside; the games are built around what block producers actually do. 👤
- **Free play is possible.** Koinos's Mana model lets the platform pay players' transaction costs through the Mana sponsor, so anyone — even an empty wallet or a guest on a phone — can play without fees (D-053, D-009). 👤
- **On-chain over servers.** As much as possible runs on chain, so there are no hosting or API bills and results can be checked by anyone (D-005). 👤
- **To add to the ecosystem:** a "Built on Koinos" section that also lists the founder's other builds, such as SwarmTales. 👤
- Koinos is small enough that one dedicated builder can make a visible difference. 👤

## Why VHP
- **VHP is what produces blocks on Koinos.** Staking VHP in the vault puts it to work as the **Knode Games Pool** (D-073); rewards come from that real production. 👤
- **No one wagers their stake.** Prizes and staked rewards come from block production — never from other players losing money. Stakes stay withdrawable at any time (D-016, D-075). 👤
- **KOIN becomes VHP.** KOIN can be burned into VHP on deposit; rewards and claims are paid in VHP. 👤
- **The swap desk turns production into liquidity:** VHP in, KOIN out, refilled by its own node — "burn without burning" (D-044). 👤

## Long-term vision
- **A growing family of node-based games** under one vault, each with its own leaderboard; players stake in as many as they like (D-074). 👤
- **Mobile free play** as a first-class experience — an installable web app with a guest wallet, no app stores and no wallet of our own (top priority, "massive missing link"). 👤
- **Producers known by name** across every game (D-030). 👤
- **Self-sustaining at $0 running cost,** with tasteful, non-intrusive ads for revenue (no adult content). 👤
- **The founder plays too** — staking a larger amount of their own VHP as a regular player once satisfied it's safe. 👤
- **Community backing** — hoped-for seed support from a Koinos fund. 👤
- Left open for later: a Knode Games token (D-072), Mana Station (D-053). ~~Free boards for the staked-only games (D-071)~~ — decided 3 Nov: every game has Free play; free prizes are switched on per game when the seed allows (D-081, D-082).

## Core principles
1. **Funds are never at risk.** Security first, then correctness and accuracy. 👤 (D-003, D-016)
2. **Fair and honest.** A small, flat fee (5% — the founder dropped a 2% owner share as less fair). Our own nodes play in the open market, and we say so. 👤
3. **Not gambling.** No wagers, odds or player-funded pots; stakes are never at risk (D-075). 👤
4. **Free to play.** Anyone can play without spending Mana; free players can win small prizes. 👤
5. **Simple.** Easy, functional, nothing confusing that makes someone close the page; stake, withdraw and claim in a couple of taps. 👤
6. **Never rushed.** Picks can be made well ahead; nothing locks with too little time. 👤
7. **Fun.** Pages feel inviting, exciting — a "get hooked" vibe, not plain. 👤
8. **Only the best version ships.** Nothing is announced until it's built, audited and ready (D-064). 👤
9. **On time, every time.** Questions release at 12:00 UTC on the dot — the site's integrity depends on it (D-047). 👤

## What success looks like
- Every game live in its best, audited version, all paying from one safe vault (D-052, D-064). 👤
- Players growing week over week — launch week for testing, then weeks spent growing the player count. 👤
- More VHP staked and more real activity on Koinos, from players who contribute. 👤
- The founder comfortable staking their own VHP as a player. 👤
- Running costs at $0, with ad revenue on top. 👤
- **First target: over 1,000,000 VHP staked in the Knode Games Pool** (the vault). More is welcome; 1,000,000 is the first milestone (D-076). 👤
- **Free play outgrowing its budget:** having to enlarge the Mana sponsor because demand is overwhelming is a sign of success (D-076). 👤

## What failure looks like
The opposite of the principles above (confirmed by the founder, 3 Nov).
- Any player losing funds, or a stake that can't be withdrawn.
- The vault owing more than it holds.
- Players experiencing it as gambling, or feeling they lost money by playing.
- A game that looks rigged — hidden advantages for our own nodes or a rule changed after picks.
- Free prizes farmed by empty wallets instead of reaching real players (I-035).
- A confusing page that makes people leave.
- Announcing something broken, or questions that come out late.
- Costs creeping up, or the platform depending on paid servers.
