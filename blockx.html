Who signs most often

How it works

The targets. From Mon 9 Nov 2026, every block whose number is a multiple of 1,200 is a target, about every hour (before that, every 2,400 blocks). About a day of targets is always open. Nobody chooses them.

The lock. The Picks contract refuses a pick once the chain reaches 100 blocks before the target, so every pick is in before anyone can know who signs it. You can change a pick once, and make up to 48 picks a day (a change counts).

The score. Koinos picks who signs each block in proportion to VHP, so big producers sign more often. A right pick scores

min(5, top producer's VHP ÷ your producer's VHP)

The cap is ×5 from Mon 9 Nov and was ×3 before.

with the VHP from the Producer Race's hourly handicap card at the lock. A wrong pick scores 0. Ties on the board go to more right picks, then the earlier last right pick.

Prizes. Two boards, as in every Knode Games game: Staked (1 VHP or a Knode Games stake) and Free play (everyone else, guests too). Each week the top 2 of Free play (picks on at least 3 days) win 2 and 1 VHP. The Staked board shares Block X's weekly reward bucket. Prizes and bucket shares are paid from Week 1 (the week of Mon 16 Nov); winners claim them from the vault. Targets count for the week Monday to Saturday (UTC). Sunday is review day: results are checked and paid from Monday. Sunday's targets are fun rounds that count on Today and All time, not the week (from Week 1). A week is final from Monday 00:00 UTC, once its results read the same on two checks an hour apart.

Fair checks. Results come from irreversible blocks that match on two independent nodes, and the scoring code is public: the page and the recorder run the same file.
