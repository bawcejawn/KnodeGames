Producer Bracket

A knockout of Koinos block producers, head to head, like March Madness. Fill in every winner by Monday night; then one round is played each day on real blocks, and the final is on Saturday. 8, 16 or 32 producers, depending on how many are producing. Free to play; stake VHP in the Bracket to share its weekly rewards.

Leaderboard

Ranked by points; ties go to a right champion, then more right picks, then whoever sent their bracket first (the time of its last change). Brackets sent in the same block share a place, so a copy sent in the same block as the original ties it. The all-time board shares ties. Two boards: Staked (wallets with at least the vault's minimum stake, 1 VHP today, in the Bracket for the week; a wallet that withdrew during the week stays on this board but earns nothing) and Free play (everyone else, guests too). The week's rewards go to the Staked board by the standard Knode Games rules: the top 30% with at least 1 point. The Free play board pays no VHP prize for now. If a free prize is switched on later, this page says so first.

How it works

The week. Seeding on Sunday 00:00 UTC, picks until Monday 23:55 UTC, one round a day, the final on Saturday. Sunday is the review day: the week's results are checked on chain and go final from Monday 00:00 UTC, and rewards can be claimed from Monday evening (Pacific time).

The field. At seeding, every producer's exact VHP is averaged over the day before (about 28,800 blocks), read from the chain on two nodes. A producer's seeding VHP is the lower of that average and its average at the last seeding (a producer that wasn't there counts zero), so VHP lent to a producer, or moved to a new address, for one day buys nothing. The producers expected to sign at least 10 blocks in a 6-hour race make the field, biggest first. The bracket is the largest of 8, 16 or 32 that fits: 32 or more play Tuesday to Saturday, 16 to 31 Wednesday to Saturday, 8 to 15 Thursday to Saturday. Under 8, there is no bracket that week.

Your pick. Fill in every winner, from the first round to the champion, between Sunday 00:00 and Monday 23:55 UTC. The whole bracket goes to the chain as one number in one transaction; you can change it until the lock.

A matchup. Each round is played on one UTC day. A producer's index is the blocks it signed that day ÷ the blocks it was expected to sign from the effective VHP it held as the day began, rebuilt exactly from the chain's own VHP records. Koinos picks each block's signer in proportion to VHP, so a producer that keeps its VHP and signs its fair share scores about 100% whatever its size: a small producer can beat a big one. A block signed while holding more than the starting VHP counts for less, in proportion, so VHP added during the day neither helps nor hurts. The higher index goes through; an exact tie goes to the lower seed.

Forfeits. A seed whose effective VHP, averaged over its round's day, is less than half its seeding VHP loses that matchup, whatever its blocks. Moving VHP away for a moment changes nothing; throwing a matchup costs a whole day of real VHP. If both seeds are under half, the blocks decide. No rule can stop a producer from losing on purpose (for example by signing from a second address of its own); it can never make itself win.

The Knode Games Pool. The vault, whose VHP is players' stakes, produces blocks as the Knode Games Pool and plays like any producer. Players can deposit and withdraw at any time, so it never forfeits, and it is judged on a rate that no deposit or withdrawal can move: each block it signs counts 1 ÷ its VHP at that block, against the same expected total as everyone's index.

Points. A right winner scores 1 point in the first round, then 2, 4, 8 (and 16 in a 32-producer bracket), plus 2 for the right champion.

Fair checks. Every hour of every round is recorded from irreversible blocks that match on two independent Koinos nodes, and read again in a later run before the week can go final. The publish job works every result and every point out again on its own before anything is published, and the scoring code is public: this page and the recorder run the same file. If the week's results or stakes can't be completed by Thursday 00:00 UTC after it, the week closes without rewards and the vault rolls them over.
