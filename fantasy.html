What these numbers mean
Cost
What the producer takes from your budget of 100. Set before the week starts and fixed all week.
Form
Its average day over the 7 days before the prices were made, measured by the VHP Ruler. 100 means it signed every block it was due. Cost follows it: about 20 for a typical producer, up to 40, down to 8.
History
How many of those 7 days count. A day the producer was void (its VHP moved outside the ruler's band) is left out. Fewer than 7 shows Little history: its price is less certain.
Swings
A small producer is due only a few blocks a day, so one block more or less swings its score a lot (3 when due 2 is 150; none is 0). A big producer stays near 100 unless it misses blocks.

Picking tip: above 100 is mostly luck and tends not to last. Below 90 usually means the node missed blocks. Cheap, steady producers near 100 are often the best value.

Questions

How often can I change my team, or my captain?

Once a day. Your one change is either swapping one producer for another, or moving the C to a different producer. So you can move your captain every day, as long as that's your only change that day. A producer you swap in can become captain in the same change. The team sheet shows how many changes you have left.

What happens if I don't save anything?

Your last saved team keeps playing, every day, until you change it. You never have to log in daily.

When does my team lock?

5 minutes before each day starts. The countdown at the top shows the next lock. Live game days are UTC days: they start at 5:00pm Pacific (4:00pm in winter).

Can I set my team for later days now?

Yes. Use the day buttons on the team sheet to plan any day up to a week ahead. You can still change a planned day until its own lock.

Which days count?

Monday to Saturday: 6 play days. Sunday is review day: results are checked and paid from Monday. On Sunday your Saturday team plays a fun round: it counts on the Today and All time boards, never the week. It's also the time to line up next week's team (its prices are out from Saturday). The week goes final from Monday 00:00 UTC, once two checks an hour apart agree.

What's a new week?

Each week starts on Monday with new prices, so Monday's team can be any five that fit the budget, with no limit on changes. Prices come out two days before.

How many players can I challenge?

One rival a week. You can change who until the week's first lock. Any number of players can challenge you. A match counts when you are both staked in Fantasy that week and both score. Whoever scores more that week wins. Rival is for bragging rights: no prize.

Why does a producer show 150, or 0?

Points compare the blocks it signed with the blocks it was due. A small producer is due only a few blocks, so luck moves it a lot: 3 signed when 2 were due is 150, and none is 0. Your team scores at most 150 from any producer in a day.

How do I win?

Your week's points are the total of your five producers' day scores, with the captain counted twice. Fantasy is a staked game: the top 30% of Fantasy stakers each week share Fantasy's part of the vault's weekly reward.

Full rules

The week. Teams play Monday to Saturday, 6 days. Sunday is review day: results are checked and paid from Monday. Sunday is a fun round: your Saturday team plays on and scores on Today and All time only, not the week (no picks needed). It's the time to line up next week's team. The week goes final from Monday 00:00 UTC, once the stakes are read and two checks an hour apart agree.

Your team. Five different producers from the week's price list, within a budget of 100 (the salary cap). The first one is your captain and scores double. Save a team for the next day; it carries on until you change it.

A fresh start every week. New prices come out before each week, and Monday's team can be any team that fits them. Last week's team carries into Monday only if it still fits (all five on the list, within 100).

Then one change a day. From one day to the next you may swap one producer, or move the captain's armband. The producer you bring in can also take the armband as part of that same swap, from whichever producer had it. A team with more changes, over the budget, or with a producer not on the list is ignored, and yesterday's team plays.

Points. Koinos picks who signs each block in proportion to VHP, so every producer has a fair share of blocks. Each day a producer scores

100 × blocks it signed ÷ blocks it was expected to sign

(at most 150), both measured by the VHP Ruler for that UTC day: the expected blocks follow the producer's VHP block by block. 100 means it signed exactly its share; a node that misses blocks, or is offline, scores less, and that counts in its form. Void: if a producer's VHP moves outside the ruler's band for the day (down more than 12% or up more than 50%), it scores 0 that day only, and the day is left out of its form. Your week is the sum of your 6 play days; a day before you join scores 0.

Prices. The price list comes out Saturday 00:00 UTC (Friday evening in the Americas) for the next week, two days before it starts, and never changes after that. The list holds every producer that passes the VHP Ruler's form gate (it signed at least 80% of its share over the last 14 days, or signs steadily). A producer's price follows its form over the 7 days from the Friday to the Thursday before: 20 for the middle of the pool, up to 40 for the most reliable, down to 8 for the least. Its fingerprint (sha256) is checked on every run, so nobody can change it later.

Plan ahead. Once the prices are out you can line up your team, and a change for any later day of the week, all at once. A planned day can still be changed until its own lock, so planning early never ties your hands. If changing an earlier day would make a planned one break the one-change rule, the page tells you.

Locks. The Picks contract refuses a team 5 minutes before its UTC day starts, so nobody can pick knowing the blocks. Picking late gives no edge on prices (they're fixed) or on VHP moves (each producer's expected share follows its VHP block by block).

Prizes. Wallets with at least 1 VHP staked in Fantasy that week play on the Staked board: the Fantasy share of the Knode Games vault's weekly reward goes to the top 30% of them with at least 1 point. Everyone else plays on the Free play board, ranked on its own. The Free play board pays no VHP for now. The week goes final from Monday 00:00 UTC, once two checks on two nodes agree.

Weekly Rival. Challenge one player for the coming week. Whoever scores more that week wins. Your record counts the challenges you made and those you received. A match counts only if both of you are staked in Fantasy and both score, so nobody piles up wins against throwaway wallets.

All time. Every finished week adds your points to the all-time table: total, weeks played and best week. Bragging rights only; prizes are weekly.

Picks are public. Teams are saved on the Koinos chain, so anyone can see them before the lock. That is how it works for now; hidden-until-lock picks may come later.

Fair checks. Days are scored from irreversible blocks that match on two independent nodes, with the VHP Ruler's own code, and every card is rebuilt and checked before results are published. The page and the recorder run the same rules file (games/fantasy/math.js).
